# Famous Dyslexic People — WordBloom Encouragement Bank

## For reading struggles
- Agatha Christie: wrote 66 novels and said she found reading hard her whole life
- Richard Branson: failed school exams and could not read until he was 8, built Virgin empire
- Whoopi Goldberg: thought she was dumb for years, became an Oscar winner

## For writing struggles
- Steven Spielberg: called lazy and stupid at school, directed Jaws and Schindler's List
- Albert Einstein: could not read until age 9, changed how the world understands physics

## For speed challenges
- Simone Biles: processes information differently, became the greatest gymnast in history

## For children specifically
- Orlando Bloom: had dyslexia and worked with a tutor every day, became a film star
- Erin Brockovich: struggled at school, became one of the most famous legal campaigners ever

## For when a child wants to give up
- Walt Disney: was told he had no imagination and was fired from his first job
- Thomas Edison: his teacher said he was too stupid to learn anything, invented the lightbulb
